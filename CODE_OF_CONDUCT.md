# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome contributors regardless of experience, background, identity, language, or location. Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and make room for newcomers to ask questions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

Technical disagreement is expected. Critique the implementation, evidence, or tradeoff—not the person. Because this project works with public records, contributors must also avoid sensationalizing personal information or using project spaces to target people named in official documents.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the primary maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.

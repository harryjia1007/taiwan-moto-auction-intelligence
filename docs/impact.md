## Public-interest value

The project addresses a structural access problem: official Taiwanese motorcycle-auction facts are split across incompatible search pages, procurement listings, PDFs, and agency sites. A reusable evidence-preserving pipeline can reduce manual searching while keeping users close to the original official records.

The most important outcome is not a high listing count. It is the ability to reproduce a normalized fact, distinguish unknown from negative data, retain historical auction rounds, and detect when a source has changed or failed.

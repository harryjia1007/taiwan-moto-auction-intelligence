# Source Families

Government e-Procurement asset sales use official dataset 7263 XML under OGDL 1.0; the MOJ centralized seized-property portal has a separate public read-only adapter and access policy. Judicial Yuan district-court execution is `MANUAL_ONLY`: a human reviews official PDF links and structured fields, then the importer stores the normalized row and publisher URL without querying, opening, downloading, or mirroring the blocked central site. The separate `judicial_notices` source performs a bounded robots-first search of the Judicial Yuan main site's other-notices section; it is supplemental and never implies full 22-court coverage. Administrative Enforcement has two deliberately separate paths: 13 branch CMS sites provide bounded automatic announcement discovery, while the CAPTCHA-gated central search accepts human-saved result HTML for offline parsing or a human-created detail manifest. Customs reads the four official HTML announcement channels and leaves restricted attachments on the official server.

Administrative Enforcement automation stops at CAPTCHA or authentication; branch CMS discovery never calls the central search. Branch CMS may inspect at most 12 recent generic auction-title detail pages per branch and accepts one only when its HTML body explicitly proves both auction and vehicle semantics; excess candidates are reported as partial coverage. Government e-Procurement discovers only XML titles that explicitly identify cars, motorcycles, or vehicles, and its same-host detail HTML is preserved before classification; MOJ centralized artifacts follow their separate host-scoped policy. A generic notice whose vehicle identity exists only in an unreviewed attachment is retained as a source limitation and is not invented as a vehicle record.

PCC records are classified by official wording: public asset disposal, scrap disposal, impounded/unclaimed, criminal seizure/forfeiture, or Customs. The publishing agency name alone never turns a notice into a judicial execution auction.

Historical central Judicial records came from the official all-court movable-property query. New central-source records must come from a replacement official machine-readable feed, an authorized export, or manually supplied official document URLs; main-site supplemental notices remain identified as their own source. The public UI links to the publisher's complete notice rather than exposing a private cached PDF; unexposed price, qualification, road-registration, and condition fields remain unknown.

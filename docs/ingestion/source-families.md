# Source Families

Government e-Procurement asset sales and Judicial Yuan district-court execution auctions now have public read-only adapters and remain `PARTIAL` until a successful database-backed live sync. Administrative Enforcement Agency, prosecutors, direct police/traffic pages, and Customs remain `PLANNED`; their registry rows describe ownership and expected discovery but contain no adapter implementation claims.

Administrative Enforcement automation must stop at CAPTCHA or authentication and support manually seeded official URLs. Government e-Procurement and MOJ CMS implementations must preserve and parse attachments before classifying listings.

PCC records are classified by official wording: public asset disposal, scrap disposal, impounded/unclaimed, criminal seizure/forfeiture, or Customs. The publishing agency name alone never turns a notice into a judicial execution auction.

Judicial records are discovered through the official central all-court movable-property query. Structured result rows and announcement PDFs are both retained; unexposed price, qualification, road-registration, and condition fields remain unknown.

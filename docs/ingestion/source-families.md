# Source Families

Government e-Procurement asset sales use official dataset 7263 XML under OGDL 1.0; the MOJ centralized seized-property portal has a separate public read-only adapter and access policy. Judicial Yuan district-court execution is `MANUAL_ONLY`: a human reviews official PDF links and structured fields, then the importer stores the normalized row and publisher URL without querying or mirroring the blocked central site. Administrative Enforcement has two deliberately separate paths: 13 branch CMS sites provide bounded automatic announcement discovery, while the CAPTCHA-gated central search accepts only a human-created manifest. Customs reads the four official HTML announcement channels and leaves restricted attachments on the official server.

Administrative Enforcement automation stops at CAPTCHA or authentication; branch CMS discovery never calls the central search. Government e-Procurement discovers only XML titles that explicitly identify cars, motorcycles, or vehicles, and its same-host detail HTML is preserved before classification; MOJ centralized artifacts follow their separate host-scoped policy. A generic notice whose vehicle identity exists only in an unreviewed attachment is retained as a source limitation and is not invented as a vehicle record.

PCC records are classified by official wording: public asset disposal, scrap disposal, impounded/unclaimed, criminal seizure/forfeiture, or Customs. The publishing agency name alone never turns a notice into a judicial execution auction.

Historical Judicial records came from the official central all-court movable-property query. New live records must come from a replacement official machine-readable feed, an authorized export, or manually supplied official document URLs. The public UI links to the publisher's complete notice rather than exposing a private cached PDF; unexposed price, qualification, road-registration, and condition fields remain unknown.

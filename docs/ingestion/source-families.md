# Source Families

Government e-Procurement asset sales and the MOJ centralized seized-property portal have public read-only adapters but are governed independently by the current access registry. Judicial Yuan district-court execution is `MANUAL_ONLY`: a human reviews official PDF links and structured fields, then the importer stores the normalized row and publisher URL without querying or mirroring the blocked central site. Administrative Enforcement has a CAPTCHA-safe manual discovery adapter: a person performs the official search and exports detail URLs, after which documents and photos are fetched asynchronously. Direct police/traffic pages and Customs remain `PLANNED`.

Administrative Enforcement automation stops at CAPTCHA or authentication and supports manually seeded official detail URLs. Government e-Procurement and MOJ centralized implementations preserve attachments before classifying listings. A generic “vehicle” notice whose motorcycle identity exists only in a scanned attachment is retained as a source limitation and is not invented as a motorcycle record.

PCC records are classified by official wording: public asset disposal, scrap disposal, impounded/unclaimed, criminal seizure/forfeiture, or Customs. The publishing agency name alone never turns a notice into a judicial execution auction.

Historical Judicial records came from the official central all-court movable-property query. New live records must come from a replacement official machine-readable feed, an authorized export, or manually supplied official document URLs. The public UI links to the publisher's complete notice rather than exposing a private cached PDF; unexposed price, qualification, road-registration, and condition fields remain unknown.
